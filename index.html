About Dr. Gupta

A kidney specialist who plans for the long run — with you.

Kidney disease is rarely a one-visit problem. Dr. Sachin Gupta combines a grounding in internal medicine with specialist training in nephrology to look after the whole person — blood pressure, sugar, heart and kidneys together.

Areas of expertise

Complete care for the kidneys

From the first abnormal report to transplant and beyond.

Chronic Kidney Disease

Slowing progression with blood-pressure, sugar and diet control, and planning ahead for every stage of CKD.

Haemodialysis

Safe, comfortable dialysis with regular adequacy checks, access care and a plan built around your week.

Kidney Transplantation

Pre-transplant work-up, donor evaluation and lifelong post-transplant follow-up and immunosuppression.

Peritoneal Dialysis

Home-based dialysis — training, catheter care and monitoring so treatment fits your life.

Acute Kidney Injury

Rapid diagnosis and treatment of sudden loss of kidney function, including in ICU and hospital settings.

Glomerular Diseases

Evaluation of protein or blood in urine, nephrotic and nephritic syndromes, with biopsy-guided treatment.

Electrolyte Disorders

Diagnosis and correction of sodium, potassium, calcium and acid–base imbalances.

Kidney & Hypertension

Managing high blood pressure that damages kidneys — and kidney problems that raise blood pressure.

Common conditions

Conditions Dr. Gupta treats every day

CKD

Chronic Kidney Disease

Gradual loss of kidney function over months or years.

AKI

Acute Kidney Injury

A sudden drop in kidney function that needs urgent care.

HTN

Hypertension

High blood pressure — a leading cause and result of kidney disease.

DKD

Diabetic Kidney Disease

Kidney damage from long-standing diabetes.

KTx

Kidney Transplant

Evaluation, transplant planning and lifelong follow-up.
