About Dr. Sachin Gupta

Specialist kidney care, built on 13+ years of practice.

Consultant Nephrologist & Kidney Transplant Physician, and founder of Hope Kidney Care in Thane.

Medicine first. Then the kidneys.

Dr. Sachin Gupta trained in general medicine before specialising in nephrology. That background shapes how he practises: kidney disease is treated alongside the blood pressure, diabetes, heart health and everyday routines that affect it.

Over more than 13 years, Dr. Gupta has cared for adults at every stage of kidney disease — from an unexpected creatinine report to long-term dialysis and kidney transplantation. He consults at his own clinic, Hope Kidney Care in Thane, and is attached to seven hospitals across Thane, Dombivli and Navi Mumbai, which allows him to follow patients from OPD to ward to dialysis unit.

His areas of focus include chronic kidney disease, haemodialysis and peritoneal dialysis, acute kidney injury, glomerular diseases, electrolyte disorders and kidney transplantation.

Approach to care

How Dr. Gupta looks after patients

Explain, then decide

Every report and option is explained clearly, so patients and families can take part in each decision.

Protect what's left

Early, steady control of blood pressure, sugar, diet and medicines to preserve kidney function for as long as possible.

Plan ahead

When dialysis or transplant may be needed, planning starts early — so the next step is calm, not an emergency.

Kidney disease is a long journey. No patient should walk it without a plan — or without hope.
