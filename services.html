Services & patient care

Specialist kidney care for adults.

Consultation, dialysis and vascular access — with continuity from the clinic to the hospital and back home.

01 · Consultation

Nephrology consultation

A complete assessment of your kidney health — history, examination, reports and medicines — ending with a clear, written plan.

  • New kidney problemsRaised creatinine, protein or blood in urine, swelling, reduced urine output.
  • Ongoing CKD careStaging, diet and medicine review, anaemia and bone-health management.
  • BP & diabetes with kidney involvementMedicines chosen to protect the kidneys as well as control numbers.

Bring to your consultation

  • Recent blood tests (creatinine, urea, electrolytes, haemoglobin)
  • Urine reports and any ultrasound or scan reports
  • Discharge summaries from earlier hospital stays
  • All current medicines, including over-the-counter and herbal

Two kinds of dialysis

Haemodialysis filters the blood through a machine, usually three times a week at a dialysis unit.

Peritoneal dialysis (PD) uses the lining of the abdomen as a natural filter and can be done at home after training.

02 · Dialysis

Haemodialysis & peritoneal dialysis

When the kidneys can no longer keep up, dialysis takes over their work. Dr. Gupta helps you choose the right type and keeps it safe and effective over time.

  • Planned startCounselling and access creation before dialysis is urgently needed.
  • Regular adequacy checksBlood tests and dose adjustments so dialysis keeps working well.
  • Emergency dialysisFor acute kidney injury and critical illness at attached hospitals.
03 · Vascular access

Perm cath insertion

A perm cath (tunnelled dialysis catheter) is a soft tube placed in a large neck vein and tunnelled under the skin, giving reliable access for haemodialysis over weeks to months.

  • When it's usedWhile a fistula matures, or when longer-term access is needed quickly.
  • Care afterwardsKeep the site clean and dry; report fever, redness or discharge promptly.

Performed at attached hospitals

Perm cath insertions are done in a hospital setting under local anaesthesia. Call the clinic to plan the procedure and the hospital most convenient for you.

04 · Transplant

Kidney transplantation

For many people with kidney failure, a transplant offers the best quality of life. As a kidney transplant physician, Dr. Gupta prepares patients and donors for transplant and looks after the new kidney for life.

Areas of expertise

Specialities

Chronic Kidney Disease

Slowing progression with blood-pressure, sugar and diet control, and planning ahead for every stage of CKD.

Haemodialysis

Safe, comfortable dialysis with regular adequacy checks, access care and a plan built around your week.

Kidney Transplantation

Pre-transplant work-up, donor evaluation and lifelong post-transplant follow-up and immunosuppression.

Peritoneal Dialysis

Home-based dialysis — training, catheter care and monitoring so treatment fits your life.

Acute Kidney Injury

Rapid diagnosis and treatment of sudden loss of kidney function, including in ICU and hospital settings.

Glomerular Diseases

Evaluation of protein or blood in urine, nephrotic and nephritic syndromes, with biopsy-guided treatment.

Electrolyte Disorders

Diagnosis and correction of sodium, potassium, calcium and acid–base imbalances.

Kidney & Hypertension

Managing high blood pressure that damages kidneys — and kidney problems that raise blood pressure.

When to see a nephrologist

Signs your kidneys may need attention

Kidney disease is often silent early on. See a nephrologist if you notice any of these — or if you have diabetes or high blood pressure and haven't had your kidneys checked.

Swelling of feet, ankles or face
Foamy or frothy urine
Blood in urine, or much less urine
High BP that's hard to control
Raised creatinine or urea on a report
Unusual tiredness, poor appetite, nausea
Diabetes for 5+ years
Family history of kidney disease
Patients treated: adults. For children with kidney problems, please consult a paediatric nephrologist.
